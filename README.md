# Ragdoll Storm

## About the game
Ragdoll Storm is pure physics-fueled mayhem: fling a wobbly, gloriously floppy
ragdoll through obstacle-course arenas and watch the world react. Every launch is a
new experiment — bounce off bumpers, crash through barriers, and ride momentum into
ever more ridiculous tumbles. The physics are weighty and expressive, so even a
failed throw is funny, and a great one feels like a tiny action movie.

Rounds are lightning fast and endlessly replayable: chase a farther fling, a
messier crash, or a cleaner line through the hazards, then launch again. The
controls are one finger simple, the feedback is juicy, and the ragdoll's flailing
never gets old. It's the perfect five-second-gameplay loop that turns into
"okay, ONE more".

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet
- **Touch and drag** anywhere to grab and fling the ragdoll — direction and speed
  of your swipe set the launch.
- **Release** to let it fly.

### Desktop
- **Left-click and drag** the ragdoll to aim your fling, **release** to launch.
> Trackpad: single click = same as left-click.
